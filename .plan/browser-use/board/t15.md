## Parent

#176 — WebUI Browser Use specification

## What to build

When the extension's connection drops, two different things have to happen. The operation that was
in flight has to end — honestly, without pretending the result is known — and the connection has to
keep trying to come back while the browser is still enabled.

The hard rule is that neither of them may quietly change what the Agent is working on. A dropped
connection must never turn into a different tab, a different browser or the isolated surface.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] If the extension disconnects during an operation, the runtime retries the connection within a
      bounded deadline of 15 seconds, using the pinned exponential backoff schedule.
- [ ] The retrying operation keeps its exact browser and tab target throughout. It never switches
      target, browser, or surface.
- [ ] When the deadline expires, the operation returns an explicit error and is not replayed, because
      whether it took effect is uncertain.
- [ ] The failure is reported as a connection failure, and is distinguishable from a
      target-not-found or a no-such-element error.
- [ ] While the browser switch remains on, background reconnection continues with a backoff capped
      at 30 seconds, until the extension reconnects or the switch is turned off.
- [ ] Turning the switch off stops the background reconnection.
- [ ] After the connection returns, the previously selected target is still selected.
- [ ] The behaviour is proven with a synthetic peer that drops the connection mid-operation and
      reconnects after it.

## Source

- ADR 0019 — the 15-second operation deadline, the 250 ms / 500 ms / 1 s / 2 s / 4 s backoff capped
  at 5 s with jitter, the explicit error without uncertain replay, the retained target, the
  background reconnection capped at 30 seconds, and the rule never to switch silently to another
  tab, run, browser or isolated surface.
