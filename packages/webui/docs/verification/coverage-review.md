## What was verified separately

The programme's closing evidence is three separate things, and none of them stands in for another:

- **Adopted contracts** — each has its own gate and its own tests: `check:webui-presentation` (classification), `check:webui-motion` (every duration and keyframe named), `check:webui-overlays` (every floating layer decided, `--strict`), `check:webui-feedback` (the alert ratchet), plus the unit and browser suites for the field, button, page and feedback contracts.
- **Accessibility obligations** — `rendering-integrity.spec.mjs` runs axe over the 48 enumerated states in both themes and compares against the recorded debt; exceptions and manual reviews are separate files with owners and removal conditions.
- **Rendering-integrity evidence** — the same 48 states are measured geometrically against a recording taken before the migration, so a squeezed, truncated or reflowed element fails rather than being recorded as "unchanged".
